# 📋 Feature Specification: Warm Heart Cafe and Bakery Web Experience

## 1. Overview
Warm Heart Cafe and Bakery is a complete 5-page digital storefront designed for artisan bakeries and boutique cafes. It showcases freshly brewed specialty coffees, handcrafted pastries, warm bakery stories, an interactive photo gallery, and direct customer contact forms.

## 2. User Stories
- **US-1 (Storefront Discovery)**: As a new visitor, I want to explore the homepage to discover featured coffee roasts and freshly baked pastries with intuitive calls-to-action.
- **US-2 (Menu Exploration)**: As a patron, I want to view categorized drink and food menus (Espresso, Cold Brews, Croissants, Artisan Bread) with transparent pricing and descriptions.
- **US-3 (Brand Heritage)**: As a community customer, I want to read the cafe's story and baking philosophy on the About page.
- **US-4 (Visual Gallery)**: As a prospective guest, I want to view high-quality bakery photography and cafe ambience in a responsive photo grid.
- **US-5 (Inquiry & Reservation)**: As a customer, I want to submit feedback, questions, or catering inquiries via an accessible contact form with instant submission alerts.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Responsive navigation bar with hamburger menu toggle on screens under 768px.
- [x] Unified color scheme with cozy warm tones (coffee browns, warm creams, bakery accents).
- [x] 5 dedicated, interconnected HTML pages (`index.html`, `about.html`, `menu.html`, `gallery.html`, `contact.html`).
- [x] Contact form event listener intercepts submit, displays confirmation, and resets fields without reload.
- [x] 1-Click launcher (`run_website.bat`) allows effortless instant launch in browser or local server.
